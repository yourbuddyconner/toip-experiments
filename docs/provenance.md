# Provenance

A lighter form of zk-pdp's rules (`~/code/zk-pdp/docs/provenance.md`), for an ideation repository.

- **Sources.** Each thing read gets a note in `docs/sources/`: what, when, how it was read, and a trust level (primary, secondary, or summary only). Facts about the stack cite a source note.
- **Notebook.** One file per working day in `docs/notebook/`, append-only. What was done, decided and left open.
- **Decisions.** A choice that changes direction gets a numbered record in `docs/decisions/`: context, options, decision, who proposed and who accepted.
- **Ours or theirs.** Mark ideas as ours, and claims about the stack as theirs, with a source. A guess is labeled a guess.
- **Code.** Prefer depending on zk-pdp at a pinned commit over copying it. Anything copied says where from and at which commit.
