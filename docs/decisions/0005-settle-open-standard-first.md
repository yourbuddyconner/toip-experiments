# 0005: Settle, the open payments standard, comes before the Commons business

Date: 2026-11-06

## Context

Exploring payments for apps with several untrusted parties led to two possible businesses:
- owning an open payments standard and being the default arbiter on it
- a vertical product, Commons for property managers, where payments are how it makes money

## Decision

The convener chose the open standard first ("I want to do the first before I do the second").

Settle is open: Trust Task types, policies, a Solana escrow program with no built-in fee, a reference processor and a reference arbiter. The business is the default arbiter, and later guarantees. Commons comes after, on top of Settle.

Crypto rails (stablecoins on Solana, Base later) were the convener's choice. Regulation is out of scope by the convener's decision. Solana first, for its Ed25519 keys, signed-message releases, and the program's shape: the assistant's calls.
